Custom Software Development FAQs

Custom software is essential when your business processes represent core competitive differentiators, when off-the-shelf SaaS cannot accommodate your complex workflows, or when licensing costs scale unsustainably with transaction volume.

100% of the source code, architecture designs, data models, and proprietary business logic belong entirely to you from day one, with full legal IP transfer explicitly specified in our Master Services Agreement.

We employ the Strangler Fig pattern to progressively migrate monolithic subsystems into independent microservices. We run traffic mirroring and bidirectional database synchronization to ensure 100% business continuity with zero downtime.

We match the tech stack to your operational demands: Go (Golang) for ultra-high concurrency and low memory overhead; Java Spring Boot for mature enterprise ERP integrations; Node.js for real-time I/O-heavy event streaming; and Python for data-intensive calculation engines.

We offer tiered SLA support packages including 24/7 incident response, proactive performance tuning, quarterly dependency updates, security patching, and ongoing sprint allocations for continuous feature enhancement.
